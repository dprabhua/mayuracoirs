Commercial Hydroponic Farm Transformation

Location: Bangalore, Karnataka | Farm Size: 5 acres

The Challenge

A commercial hydroponic farm in Bangalore was struggling with inconsistent yields and high water consumption. Their existing growing medium wasn't providing optimal conditions for plant growth, leading to lower productivity and increased operational costs.

The Solution

After consulting with our agricultural experts, the farm switched to Mayuracoirs' premium cocopeat blocks. Our cocopeat provided:

  • Superior water retention (up to 8-10 times its weight)
  • Optimal aeration for root development
  • Consistent pH levels (5.8-6.5)
  • Natural disease resistance

The Results

  • 40% increase in overall yield
  • 30% reduction in water consumption
  • 25% decrease in fertilizer costs
  • Improved crop quality and consistency
"Switching to Mayuracoirs' cocopeat has been a game-changer for our hydroponic farm. The consistent quality and excellent water retention have significantly improved our yields while reducing our operational costs." - Arun Kumar, Farm Owner

Organic Farming Success

Location: Tamil Nadu | Farm Size: 10 acres

The Challenge

A 10-acre organic farm in Tamil Nadu was facing soil degradation and poor water retention issues. The farm needed a sustainable solution to improve soil health while maintaining organic certification standards.

The Solution

We recommended a combination of our coir pith and cocopeat products to:

  • Enhance soil structure
  • Improve water retention
  • Provide natural organic matter
  • Support beneficial microbial activity

The Results

  • 50% improvement in soil structure
  • 40% increase in water retention
  • 35% higher crop yields
  • Reduced irrigation frequency
"Mayuracoirs' products have transformed our soil health. The combination of coir pith and cocopeat has given us the perfect growing medium for organic farming." - Priya Sharma, Agricultural Consultant
