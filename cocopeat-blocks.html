Usage Instructions

Simple steps to get started with cocopeat blocks

1

Soak the Block

Place the cocopeat block in a container and add water. Wait for 30 minutes until fully expanded.

2

Break and Mix

Break apart the expanded cocopeat and mix thoroughly. Add perlite or vermiculite for better aeration if needed.

3

Ready to Use

Your cocopeat is now ready for planting. Use it as a growing medium or mix with soil as needed.
