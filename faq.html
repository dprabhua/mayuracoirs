Cocopeat is a natural byproduct of coconut processing. It's made from the pith inside the coconut husk, which is processed to create a versatile growing medium. Our cocopeat is carefully processed to ensure optimal quality and consistency for various agricultural applications.

Cocopeat offers numerous benefits:

  • Excellent water retention and aeration
  • Natural pH balance
  • Eco-friendly and sustainable
  • Free from weeds and pathogens
  • Reusable and biodegradable

EC (Electrical Conductivity) measures the salt content in the growing medium:

  • High EC Products: Ideal for established plants and hydroponic systems
  • Low EC Products: Perfect for seed starting and sensitive plants

Cocopeat can last for 3-4 years when properly maintained. It's recommended to:

  • Store in a dry place
  • Keep away from direct sunlight
  • Rehydrate before use
  • Replace if it shows signs of decomposition

Cocopeat and coir pith are the same thing — both refer to the fibrous material extracted from coconut husks. "Cocopeat" is the commercial name used in horticulture and hydroponics, while "coir pith" is the technical term used in manufacturing. Both are 100% natural, biodegradable, and excellent growing media.

EC (Electrical Conductivity) measures the salt content in cocopeat. High EC cocopeat (0.8–1.2 mS/cm) is ideal for established plants and commercial crops. Low EC cocopeat (0.1–0.4 mS/cm) is better for seedlings, sensitive plants, and hydroponics where you control nutrition precisely. Choosing the right EC avoids nutrient imbalance in your crops.

For soil amendment, typically 2–3 tonnes of cocopeat per acre is recommended for the first application. For hydroponics grow bags, you need approximately 1–2 kg of cocopeat per plant depending on the crop. Contact us for a precise calculation based on your crop and field conditions.
