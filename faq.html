Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

Cocopeat is a natural byproduct of coconut processing. It's made from the pith inside the coconut husk, which is processed to create a versatile growing medium. Our cocopeat is carefully processed to ensure optimal quality and consistency for various agricultural applications.

Cocopeat offers numerous benefits:

  • Excellent water retention and aeration
  • Natural pH balance
  • Eco-friendly and sustainable
  • Free from weeds and pathogens
  • Reusable and biodegradable
  1. Place the block in a container
  2. Add water (approximately 3-4 times the block's weight)
  3. Wait for 15-20 minutes for the block to expand
  4. Break up the expanded cocopeat
  5. Mix with soil or use as a standalone growing medium

EC (Electrical Conductivity) measures the salt content in the growing medium:

  • High EC Products: Ideal for established plants and hydroponic systems
  • Low EC Products: Perfect for seed starting and sensitive plants

Cocopeat can last for 3-4 years when properly maintained. It's recommended to:

  • Store in a dry place
  • Keep away from direct sunlight
  • Rehydrate before use
  • Replace if it shows signs of decomposition
