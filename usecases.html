Key Benefits of Cocopeat

Discover why our cocopeat products are the preferred choice for modern agriculture

Water Efficiency

Retains 8-10 times its weight in water, significantly reducing irrigation frequency and promoting sustainable water usage.

Disease Resistance

Coir substrate can help obtain disease resistance in crops, providing a healthier growing environment.

Reduced Labor

Streamlines cultivation processes, making farming operations more efficient and cost-effective.

Superior Nutrient Retention & pH Control

Holds nutrients effectively and maintains a balanced pH (5.7 to 6.7), creating optimal growing conditions.

Environmental Credentials

100% organic, natural, renewable, biodegradable alternative to peat moss, contributing to peat bog preservation.

Hydroponics & Soilless Gardening

Our cocopeat products excel in hydroponic systems, providing optimal growing conditions for a wide range of crops. The superior water retention and aeration properties make it an ideal medium for soilless cultivation.

Benefits

  • Excellent water retention and drainage
  • Optimal root aeration and development
  • pH neutral and sterile medium
  • Sustainable alternative to rockwool
  • Reusable and biodegradable

Suitable Crops

  • Tomatoes and Peppers
  • Strawberries and Cucumbers
  • Lettuce and Melons
  • Carrots and Bell Peppers
  • Bananas
